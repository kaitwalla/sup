## Trial art direction

Use a 3/4 diorama camera, warm interior lighting, a few plants, one couch social area, one desk focus area, and restrained prop density.

Avoid a first-person camera, an open-plan office aesthetic, expensive realtime shadows everywhere, or hundreds of decorative objects.

The goal is a small cozy room, not a metaverse lobby.
